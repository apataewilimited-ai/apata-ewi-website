Our financial empowerment initiative

W.F.C. — Wealthy Financial Contributions.

W.F.C. digital platform · In development

A clearer way to follow your contribution journey.

The planned platform includes an Android member app and a web administration dashboard. The first planned payment workflow is bank transfer, with direct online payments considered for a later phase.

01

Member access

Designed to help members view their information and contribution activity.

02

Cycle & payout records

Rotational contribution cycles with the payout order agreed before a cycle begins.

03

Responsible approvals

Planned two-authorised-officer approval for payouts, with reconciliation and an audit trail.

04

Missed-payment process

A documented case-review and notification process, following the programme’s approved rules.

These are planned capabilities, not live app functions. Final features, contribution rules, payout procedures, and applicable regulatory requirements must be confirmed before launch. No financial returns are promised.

W.F.C. is a contribution community built around financial discipline, unity, accountability and mutual support. Its guiding idea is simple: help members build consistent habits and work towards shared goals through organised contribution cycles.

Priority hiring — Phase 1

1. Administrative / Operations Officer

Coordinates daily operations, staff records, company documents, follow-ups, reports, meetings and communication. Suggested background: OND, HND or B.Sc. in administration, management or a related field, with computer skills. Target experience in the supplied graphic: at least 2 years.

2. Finance / Accounts Officer

Records income and expenses, handles bank transactions and petty cash, prepares reports and reconciliations, manages invoices and supplier payments, and supports payroll and W.F.C. records. Suggested background: HND/B.Sc. in accounting, finance or a related field, with Excel and accounting-software skills. Target experience: at least 2 years.

3. Sales / Shop Officer

Serves customers, records sales and receipts, monitors stock, keeps the shop organised and follows up on orders. Suggested background: SSCE, OND, HND or B.Sc., good communication and customer-service skills, and basic computer/POS skills. Target experience: 1–2 years.

4. Farm / Livestock Supervisor

Supervises poultry, livestock and farm workers; feeding, watering, hygiene, farm records, health and vaccination schedules. Suggested background: HND/B.Sc. in animal science, agriculture or a related field, with livestock-management knowledge. Target experience: at least 2 years.
